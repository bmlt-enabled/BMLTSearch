# Changelog

## 6.1.0 (August 31, 2026)

- Meetings now use Apple Maps on iPhone and iPad.
- Bigger, easier-to-tap meeting pins on the map.
- Virtual meeting links now open directly in Zoom.

## 6.0.0 (August 18, 2026)

- **Now available on the web** at [app.bmlt.app](https://app.bmlt.app), and installable as an app — alongside iOS and Android.
- **In-person / online filter on Near Me** — ask for online meetings, or leave them out when only a room will do.
- **Virtual and hybrid meetings are now badged** so you can tell a meeting's type at a glance.
- **The map shows only meetings with a real venue** — online meetings are no longer pinned to arbitrary locations.
- **All regions in one place** — the Service Body List now shows every region together, so nothing is hidden behind a zone.
- **Cleaner browsing** — regions without their own meetings no longer show an empty row.
- **Clearer errors** — a failed search now says so and lets you retry, instead of showing a blank screen.
- **Easier navigation** — search modes live in a permanent bottom bar, and About moved there too.
- **Online meeting times** now show in the meeting's own timezone, clearly labeled.
